# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Local clients of every age and gender (unisex: women, men and kids) looking for an everyday hair salon nearby. They visit the site to see what the salon offers and what it costs, see who will cut their hair, and find out where and when to come in.

## Product Purpose

The public website for a single hair salon. Its job is to get people through the door: a visitor should leave knowing the services and prices, trusting the stylists, and knowing the address and opening hours. Success means more walk-in visits.

## Positioning

An affordable, friendly neighbourhood salon: good-value everyday hair care for the whole family, without a premium price or attitude. Avoid wording that implies luxury, exclusivity or specialist expertise the salon has not claimed.

## Operating Context

- Clients come in person. Walk-ins are the main way to get service, so location, directions and opening hours are the main thing the site has to make easy.
- Most visitors are likely local and on phones, checking quickly before heading out.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed features:
- Services and prices: a list of services with prices (and durations where known).
- Stylists and gallery: profiles of the team and a portfolio of hair work.
- Contact and location: address, opening hours, map and contact details.

Not in scope now:
- Online booking. Do not build or imply it unless this record changes.

Open decisions:
- Salon name, address, phone, hours and service list are all still unknown.
- Whether phone or WhatsApp contact is offered as well as walk-ins.

Technical: an existing Next.js 16 (App Router) + React 19 + Tailwind CSS 4 codebase, currently the unchanged create-next-app starter.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. There is no confirmed salon name, logo, photos, team, prices, address or reviews. Future work must use clearly marked placeholders and must never invent a business name, prices, stylist names or bios, testimonials, ratings, awards or a "years in business" figure.

## Product Principles

1. **Getting there comes first.** Address, hours and directions are never more than one step away.
2. **Prices are honest and visible.** Affordability is the promise, so show prices plainly, with no "from" tricks or hidden costs.
3. **Friendly, not fancy.** The voice is warm, plain and welcoming to every client, whatever their age or gender.
4. **Real people, real work.** Stylists and gallery images build trust only when they are genuine; use placeholders until real ones exist.
